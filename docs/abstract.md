# Proposed abstract

This proposed study will establish the requirements and design of a guardian-signed electronic promissory note workflow at Mabini Colleges, Inc. It will investigate the current local process, synthesize relevant literature, derive traceable requirements, and validate a proposed workflow through stakeholder walkthroughs of synthetic cases. Candidate participants include eligible students, participating guardians, Dean reviewers, and President's Office personnel in confirmed participating units. The intended design moves guardian signing onto the student's device within the authenticated student session before sequential institutional approval and preserves the accepted signed record. This context does not independently authenticate the guardian. The intended contribution is an evidence-supported workflow and implementation specification, including anticipated benefits, practical burdens, design revisions, and unresolved concerns. Included units, sampling, institutional permissions, and collection arrangements remain protocol decisions. Implementation and subsequent operational evaluation are separate work. No findings, completed validation, or demonstrated benefits are reported.
