# Study A Literature Search Record

## Scope and selection procedure

Search and source-check date: **6 November 2026 (Asia/Manila)**. This is a focused narrative review for [issue #3](https://github.com/frankkivenablao-mabini/guardian-signed-promissory-note-research/issues/3), covering electronic acknowledgment/signatures, student financial administration, preserved records, usability, and design science evaluation. It is not a systematic review, an exhaustive database search, or a test of novelty. No publication-year, country, language, or recency filter was applied in the search interface; only English-readable material was assessed.

Discovery used the web search tool with the exact strings below. Original sources were then opened on journal, author, university, NIST, and NARA sites; portal requirements were read through the GitHub API. No subscription database, Google Scholar search, participant records, or operational portal was accessed. Search rankings and pages can change; the queries and dated candidate decisions below permit rerunning the procedure without implying identical results.

Selection rules were applied as follows:

1. Include original research or methodology relevant to an RQ; include official technical guidance for terminology/record handling, and university instructions only as examples of administrative practice. Keep their evidence roles distinct.
2. Check authors, title, year, venue/report, DOI or stable URL, and the specific passage supporting a retained claim. Prefer original full text. If only an original abstract is accessible, retain only claims explicitly supported there and label the limit.
3. Exclude secondary summaries as claim evidence when the original is available, superseded guidance as current advice, unrelated applications, vendor promises, and unverifiable local claims. An inaccessible full text does not authorize inference from its title or a search snippet.
4. Map retained evidence and limitations to the current A-RQ1–A-RQ3 in the [matrix](literature-matrix.md). Do not convert findings from another institution, law, or technology into outcomes at Mabini Colleges, Inc.

The register records the candidates selected for source inspection and the exclusions relevant to the synthesis. Other returned hits were not individually assessed or counted. There is no PRISMA flow count, saturation claim, or assertion that all relevant studies were found.

## Limits and update procedure

Coverage is deliberately bounded: a single financial-aid experiment, methodological sources, technical guidance, and administrative examples do not represent the whole literature on Philippine student finance or electronic signing. Inaccessible full texts limit appraisal of L01–L02; university pages describe instructions rather than evaluation methods. No retained source observes this proposed Mabini guardian-signing workflow. These are limits of this search, not proof that no prior study exists.

For an update, rerun the recorded queries, add new dated searches and candidate decisions, check original passages and publication versions, and amend the matrix, synthesis, and references together. A broader database search or verified local documentary evidence may change the synthesis. Protocol decisions and institutional validation remain under #4; no source here supplies research permission or a sample size.
