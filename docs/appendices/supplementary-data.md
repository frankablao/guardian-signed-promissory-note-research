# Evidence and supplementary material plan

No study dataset, participant transcript, or actual signature is included. Maintain restricted evidence separately and publish only synthetic examples or permitted disclosure-reviewed summaries.

For each substantive claim retain: claim ID, A-RQ, source type/version, permitted evidence locator, design/mockup version, analysis decision, claim limit, and disclosure disposition. Distinguish literature, local observation, design constraint, expectation, and completed walkthrough finding. The handoff register is a starting requirements structure, not a completed evidence ledger.
