## Claim-to-question map

The [synthesis](../introduction/related-literature.md) separates literature findings, local unknowns, established constraints, proposed design choices, and anticipated benefits. Proposed applications below are researcher's inferences. A retained source does not itself validate a requirement or design.

| Question | Contextual support | Local unknown and proposed evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A-RQ1 — current needs, constraints, difficulties | L04 motivates attention to help; L09/L10 supply bounded administrative examples; L03 describes the current process as requirements. | Which steps, difficulties, variations, help and device/guardian-access needs actually occur? Authorized process documents, interviews and observations; distinguish reported from observed experience. |
| A-RQ2 — workflow, interaction, record-preservation requirements | L03 fixes scope/constraints; L05 informs context; L06/L07 delimit identity/signature claims; L08 informs preservation rationale. | Which requirements follow from permitted local evidence, literature or explicit constraints? Trace sources to requirement IDs, process/state models and mockups; label design choices and unresolved assumptions. Literature does not mandate the local order or retention period. |
| A-RQ3 — anticipated benefits, burdens, misunderstandings, revisions | L02 supplies formative framing; L04/L05 inform help/context prompts; L06/L07 inform signing explanations; L03 defines paths to walk through. | How do stakeholders interpret a versioned proposed design? Approved walkthrough observations, explanations of terms/status/signing, expected benefits and burdens, disagreements, revisions and unresolved concerns. These do not establish implemented performance. |

L01 frames the intended contribution across all three questions. Implementation tests, operational comparison, and implemented-system participant evaluation belong in [Study B](https://github.com/frankkivenablao-mabini/guardian-signed-promissory-note-evaluation); its approved baseline must precede rollout. Study A observations need compatible definitions and permitted reuse before any baseline use. No row supplies local observations, legal enforceability, institutional permission, or evidence of repayment, retention, or academic gains. The inventory/POS PDF supplies none of these claims.
