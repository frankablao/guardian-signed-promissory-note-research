# Aim and research questions

**Aim:** Establish the local needs, evidence basis, requirements, and validated design of a guardian-signed electronic promissory note workflow at Mabini Colleges, Inc. to guide implementation of mc-portal #740.

## Research questions

- **A-RQ1:** What needs, constraints, and difficulties characterize the current promissory note process in the confirmed participating units at Mabini Colleges, Inc.?
- **A-RQ2:** What workflow, interaction, and record-preservation requirements follow from local evidence, relevant literature, and the established implementation constraints?
- **A-RQ3:** What anticipated benefits, burdens, misunderstandings, and design revisions emerge from stakeholder walkthroughs of the proposed workflow?

## Objectives and intended evidence

| Question | Objective | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A-RQ1 | Validate the current process and identify local needs | Authorized process documents, interviews, and process observations |
| A-RQ2 | Derive traceable requirements and a proposed design | Verified literature, local evidence, constraint register, process/state models, and mockups |
| A-RQ3 | Assess the design formatively and record revisions | Versioned walkthrough tasks, observations, feedback, disagreements, and revision decisions |

Study A does not compare the operational performance of an implemented electronic workflow. Study B retains the original concerns about efficiency, usability/comprehension, technical integrity, and student-session signing limitations.
