# Background and local problem

For Mabini Colleges, Inc., [mc-portal #740](https://github.com/frankkivenablao-mabini/mc-portal/issues/740) describes an electronic request followed by Dean verification, printing and guardian signing, and President's Office approval. This is a requirements-derived description; current local practice, variation, and burden require investigation.

Study A asks what local needs and evidence justify the proposed redesign and how its interactions and preserved record should work. Removing a paper handoff may reduce visits or printing, but guardian/device access and earlier signing may create burden. These are questions to investigate. The [research questions](research-questions.md) and [handoff register](../design/implementation-handoff.md) separate constraints, design choices, assumptions, and anticipated benefits.

The existing literature-review PR remains separate pending work. Methodological sources in the references supply context; they do not establish local needs, novelty, or benefits.
