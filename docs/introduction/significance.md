# Intended significance

Study A is intended to provide Mabini Colleges, Inc. with traceable requirements and a validated design before implementation. Students and guardians may contribute needs around comprehension, access, and signing effort; approving staff may identify review and record requirements. These are intended uses, not demonstrated benefits.

Its research contribution will depend on the quality of local evidence, literature-grounded reasoning, and documented design revisions. Broader transferability must be qualified by institutional and participation limits. Study B will test the resulting implementation and assess outcomes separately.
