# Significance of the Study at Mabini Colleges, Inc.

The proposed study will assess whether the guardian-signed workflow is useful within the institution's participating units and observation periods. Its value remains to be evaluated; the proposal establishes no benefit.

| Local beneficiary | Intended relevance to evaluate |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible participating students | Time, completion, visits, printing, and resubmission burden (RQ1); usability and clarity of payment terms (RQ2). |
| Participating guardians | Understanding the deferred amount, deadline, and signing meaning (RQ2); device access burden and misunderstandings about student-session signing (RQ4). |
| Dean reviewers and President's Office personnel | Reviewing the same preserved signed record with authorized access and ordered approvals (RQ3), alongside usability and perceived trustworthiness (RQ2). |
| Local administrators | Evidence about workflow choices and practical limitations within the confirmed institutional scope (RQ1–RQ4). |

The [aim and objectives](research-questions.md) guide these proposed assessments. Departments, academic levels, and observation periods require protocol confirmation. Broader research may contextualize the findings but cannot establish local outcomes. Transferability beyond Mabini Colleges, Inc. will require qualification; approval speed alone cannot establish repayment, retention, or academic improvements.
