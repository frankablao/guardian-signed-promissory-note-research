# Design and Evaluation of a Guardian-Signed Electronic Promissory Note Workflow at Mabini Colleges, Inc.

**Status: proposed study and manuscript foundation at Mabini Colleges, Inc.** No implementation, approval, recruitment, collection, or benefits are established by these pages.

The study proposes design science research with artifact development, technical verification, participant evaluation, and a baseline/post-rollout comparison. Intended participants are eligible local students, participating guardians, Dean reviewers, and President's Office personnel. Departments, academic levels, sample size, and observation periods remain protocol decisions; participation by all college units is not assumed.

The proposed workflow moves guardian signing within the student's authenticated session before Dean and President's Office approval. Both offices would review the same preserved signed record. Student-session signing does not independently authenticate the guardian. Findings will be limited to the participating units and observation periods at Mabini Colleges, Inc.; broader literature supplies context rather than local outcomes.

The [research roadmap](https://github.com/frankkivenablao-mabini/guardian-signed-promissory-note-research/issues/1) identifies later work. Results and conclusions remain unavailable pending approved research and analysis.
