# Study A: requirements and design

**Requirements and Design of a Guardian-Signed Electronic Promissory Note Workflow at Mabini Colleges, Inc.**

This manuscript foundation concerns the local needs, evidence basis, proposed design, and anticipated benefits of the workflow at Mabini Colleges, Inc. It reports no completed interviews, walkthroughs, approvals, or benefits.

The [focused literature review](introduction/related-literature.md), [source matrix](appendices/literature-matrix.md), and [search history](appendices/literature-search.md) supply contextual evidence and design rationale for A-RQ1–A-RQ3. They do not establish local needs or completed formative validation.

Read the [study split](study-split.md), [questions](introduction/research-questions.md), [draft protocol](protocol.md), [implementation handoff](design/implementation-handoff.md), and [roadmap](roadmap.md). Implementation belongs in [mc-portal #740](https://github.com/frankkivenablao-mabini/mc-portal/issues/740); operational evaluation belongs in [Study B](https://github.com/frankkivenablao-mabini/guardian-signed-promissory-note-evaluation).
