**Requirements and Design of a Guardian-Signed Electronic Promissory Note Workflow at Mabini Colleges, Inc.**

This manuscript foundation concerns the local needs, evidence basis, proposed design, and anticipated benefits of the workflow at Mabini Colleges, Inc. It reports no completed interviews, walkthroughs, approvals, or benefits.
