# Planned data collection

After applicable institutional determinations and consent arrangements, validate the requirements-derived current process through document review and local interviews/observations. Record process variation and conflicts. Derive requirements, then dry-run synthetic mockups and walkthrough prompts before approved participant sessions.

Walkthroughs should cover initial requests, exact terms, guardian acceptance/signing, staff review, status/printing, errors, cancellation/replacement, and device/access limitations. Record task version, role, assistance, observed misunderstandings, self-reported expectations, and suggested revisions separately. Do not collect actual signatures for design examples. Instruments remain drafts in the [appendix](../appendices/questionnaire.md).
