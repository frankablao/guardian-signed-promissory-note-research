# Proposed Data Collection at Mabini Colleges, Inc.

No recruitment, pilot, baseline, or field collection is documented in this foundation. Applicable institutional research/data-access requirements and the protocol must be resolved before affected work proceeds.

| Research question | Proposed collection source |
| --- | --- |
| RQ1 | Event timestamps, consistent baseline records, structured observations, and explicitly labeled self-reports of visits, printing, and resubmission. |
| RQ2 | Usability tasks, comprehension questions, and participant feedback from eligible students, guardians, Dean reviewers, and President's Office personnel. |
| RQ3 | Synthetic tests of signed-term preservation, role access, approval order, invalid/duplicate/stale/concurrent actions, and legacy compatibility, tied to an artifact version. |
| RQ4 | Interviews or observations about guardian/device access and misunderstandings of signing within the student's authenticated session. |

The [protocol decisions](participants-and-data.md) must specify included units and academic levels, observation dates, sampling, consent, instrument versions, and data handling. Whole-process measurement will begin at initial request submission and include guardian waiting time; stage times will be reported separately.

## Proposed ethics and data management

Obtain applicable institutional research approval before recruiting participants or using identifiable administrative records for research. Separate research participation from access to the promissory note service, and explain that declining participation will not affect administrative decisions.

Minimize collected personal and financial data. Use coded study identifiers, restrict access to identifiable materials, and define retention and deletion arrangements. Public reproducibility materials should use synthetic data or appropriately anonymized summaries, excluding signatures, account identifiers, and identifiable financial records.

Distinguish the guardian's acceptance of payment terms from consent to participate in research. If minors are included, determine the applicable institutional requirements before recruitment.
