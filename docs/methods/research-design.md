# Research design

Study A proposes a single-institution requirements and design study informed by design science research. Local process investigation and literature synthesis will support requirements and a design artifact; formative walkthroughs will assess the proposed workflow before implementation. Initial methodological context appears in references [1–2]; the draft protocol specifies the planned activities.

The unit of design is the local promissory note workflow. The evidence units include documents, accounts/observations of process steps, and walkthrough sessions; actual participants and counts remain unestablished. A working production system and baseline/post-rollout effect analysis belong to separate implementation/evaluation work.

See the [draft protocol](../protocol.md) and [two-study boundaries](../study-split.md).
