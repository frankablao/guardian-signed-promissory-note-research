# Research repository guidance

This repository holds **Study A: Requirements and Design of a Guardian-Signed Electronic Promissory Note Workflow at Mabini Colleges, Inc.** Keep the population, problem, evaluation, and conclusions institution-specific. Broader literature supplies context, not evidence of local outcomes.

For work on the proposal, literature, methodology, instruments, study evidence, analysis, or manuscript, read and apply [.agents/skills/mabini-promissory-research/SKILL.md](.agents/skills/mabini-promissory-research/SKILL.md). Routine configuration-only changes do not need the research workflow.

Preserve the distinction between planned work and completed research. Never invent citations, institutional approvals, participants, measurements, or results. Keep identifiable student financial records, account identifiers, linkage keys, and actual signature images out of Git and published pages.

When GitHub Work is invoked, it governs issue dependencies, branch/worktree isolation, commits, verification, and PR delivery. The research skill adds domain-specific evidence checks; it does not authorize additional GitHub workflows, recruitment, deployment, or journal submission.

Current scope, questions, and evolving decisions live in `docs/study-split.md`, `docs/introduction/research-questions.md`, and `docs/protocol.md`. Study A establishes needs, requirements, and formative design validation, with a versioned implementation handoff for mc-portal #740. Study B owns implementation/outcome evaluation in `frankkivenablao-mabini/guardian-signed-promissory-note-evaluation`; its approved baseline precedes rollout. Draft documents do not establish institutional approval or completed research.

The manuscript lives in `docs/`; source material lives in `src/`. Treat the approved protocol and versioned evidence as the sources of evolving study decisions. Do not treat template prose or the reference inventory/POS paper as completed promissory-note research.
