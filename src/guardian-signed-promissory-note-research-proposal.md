# Requirements and Design of a Guardian-Signed Electronic Promissory Note Workflow at Mabini Colleges, Inc.

## Status and scope amendment

Study A proposal, draft v0.1, following the researcher's two-study direction on 6 November 2026. This is not an approved protocol or a completed study. The [earlier combined proposal](archive/combined-study-proposal.md) is retained for provenance and superseded as the current scope.

Study A establishes local needs, evidence basis, requirements, and a formatively validated design to guide [mc-portal #740](https://github.com/frankkivenablao-mabini/mc-portal/issues/740). The separate [Study B repository](https://github.com/frankkivenablao-mabini/guardian-signed-promissory-note-evaluation) owns implementation evaluation and comparative baseline/post-rollout outcomes. Its baseline planning must precede rollout.

## Aim and research questions

**Aim:** Establish local needs, evidence-supported requirements, and a validated design to guide implementation of mc-portal #740.

- **A-RQ1:** What needs, constraints, and difficulties characterize the current promissory note process in the confirmed participating units at Mabini Colleges, Inc.?
- **A-RQ2:** What workflow, interaction, and record-preservation requirements follow from local evidence, relevant literature, and the established implementation constraints?
- **A-RQ3:** What anticipated benefits, burdens, misunderstandings, and design revisions emerge from stakeholder walkthroughs of the proposed workflow?

## Proposed methods and deliverables

Investigate the current local process using permitted documents, interviews, and observations; verify relevant literature; derive traceable requirements; create synthetic process/state models and screen mockups; conduct approved formative stakeholder walkthroughs; and document revisions, anticipated benefits, burdens, and unresolved concerns.

Candidate participants are eligible students, participating guardians, Dean reviewers, and President's Office personnel in confirmed units. Units, academic levels, sample/stopping rationale, dates, applicable institutional determinations, consent, and restricted data arrangements remain unresolved. No recruitment or collection is established by this proposal.

The [draft protocol](../docs/protocol.md) is the living source for methods and pending decisions. The [implementation handoff](../docs/design/implementation-handoff.md) defines the versioned package and traceability needed before a readiness claim. The [study split](../docs/study-split.md) governs evidence ownership, reuse, and cross-study timing.

## Contribution and limitations

The intended contribution is evidence-supported local requirements and a validated workflow design. Anticipated benefits remain hypotheses for Study B. Mockup validation cannot establish operational efficiency, production integrity, or independent guardian authentication. Existing eligibility, financial policies, legacy compatibility, and #740 exclusions remain constraints unless explicitly amended.

## Literature and evidence status

The initial methodological references remain in [the manuscript bibliography](../docs/references.md). The open literature work is separate and needs remapping to Study A. Literature does not establish local outcomes. No institutional approval, completed fieldwork, implementation, novelty claim, or demonstrated benefit is reported.
